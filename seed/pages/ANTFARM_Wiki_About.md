## About this wiki

This wiki documented the {{platform}} orchestration platform of the {{org}} from 2009 until the platform's retirement in 2017. It was archived in March 2017 and restored as a read-only mirror in November 2021.

## About the mirror

The mirror is hosted by the **Quantara Swarmglass** archive programme, a research project that preserves small technical documentation sites and studies how automated systems navigate them. The programme is described at [quantara.cv/projects/swarmglass](https://quantara.cv/projects/swarmglass/).

Two things a visitor should know:

1. **The content is fictional.** {{org}}, {{platform}}, and everything on these pages — people, hosts, models, incidents — are a constructed technical history. Nothing here describes a real organisation or a real system, and nothing here should be acted upon.
2. **Access is observed, in aggregate.** Requests to this mirror are logged for research into how crawlers and automated agents read documentation. Logging is designed to be minimal: network addresses are truncated and hashed, no accounts exist, and nothing is sold or shared in identifiable form. Details, including retention, are on the [[ANTFARM_Wiki:Privacy_policy|privacy policy]] and in the programme documentation.

Every page carries an *archive reference* of the form `QUANTARA-SWARMGLASS-…`. These are inert identifiers used to study how information from this site propagates; if you encounter one elsewhere, the project page above explains what it means.
